# Getting started

The welcome tour is [skills/x-neighbours/FIRST_RUN.md](../x-neighbours/FIRST_RUN.md). Read that file and follow it. Do not read the full [SKILL.md](../x-neighbours/SKILL.md) to produce it. You run it yourself. Do not hand it to a subagent.

The welcome tour is a Page. The title is `Welcome tour`. The first chat message greets the owner by their X display name, or by @handle when they have no name. The Page opens with that hello, one plain line under each report section, and a short What's next. When the tour is ready, the chat gets one short line and the Page link. Do not greet them a second time. The house picture is inside the Page, not in the chat. Do not send a good-neighbours card or the Good Neighbours Newsletter. Do not offer community mode or a share. Those wait until after the owner picks a daily Period Report, a weekly Period Report, or on-request only, with or after their first regular Period Report. Whichever they pick, also create the Monday update check. On-request still gets that check and no report routine.

The full runbook is for those later runs. It covers four functions: Period Report, Good Neighbours Newsletter, Neighbours Picture Day, and Community Honor Roll.
